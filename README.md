## 📖 Overview

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a Diwali Sales dataset using Python. The goal is to understand customer purchasing behavior and identify key business insights that can help improve sales strategies and customer targeting.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer purchase patterns during the Diwali festival and answer important business questions such as:

- Which gender spends the most?
- Which age group contributes the highest sales?
- Which states generate the highest revenue?
- Which occupations have the highest purchasing power?
- Which product categories are most popular?

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** `Diwali_Sales_Data.csv`

The dataset contains customer demographic information and purchase details collected during the Diwali shopping season.

## 📈 Key Insights

- Female customers contributed significantly to overall sales.
- Customers aged **26–35 years** made the highest number of purchases.
- Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka generated the highest sales.
- IT, Healthcare, and Aviation professionals showed higher purchasing power.
- Food, Clothing, and Electronics were among the most purchased categories.
